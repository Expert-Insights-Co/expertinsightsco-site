Pickup and delivery

Pickup

Choose a 30-minute window, Tuesday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. You'll get a text when your order is ready.

Local delivery

Free within about 10 miles of the bakery; $15 up to 25 miles. Cakes travel in temperature-safe boxes and are delivered by our team, never a third-party service.

What the owner sees

A note from Expert Insights: customers never see this part of the site. These are the tools that keep a busy bakery organized.

A daily order list
Every cake, size, flavor and pickup time on one screen, sorted by day and ready to print for the kitchen.
Lead times enforced
A tiered cake can't be ordered for tomorrow. The calendar blocks any date the kitchen can't make.
Deposits before baking starts
Custom work is half paid when it is ordered, so a no-show doesn't cost the bakery its ingredients and time.
Delivery-area rules
Addresses outside the delivery area can't check out, and the right fee is added for the rest.
Confirmations and reminders
Customers get a confirmation and a reminder the day before, which means fewer calls asking "is my cake ready?"
Menu updates from a phone
Seasonal items, sold-out flavors and holiday pre-orders can be changed without calling a developer.

Questions

Do you have gluten-free or allergy-friendly options?

We offer gluten-free cupcakes and 6-inch cakes, made in a shared kitchen. Allergy notes can be added to every order.

Can I change my custom cake after ordering?

Yes, up to 48 hours before your pickup or delivery for single-tier cakes, and up to 7 days before for tiered cakes.

What is your cancellation policy?

Menu orders can be cancelled for a full refund up to 24 hours ahead. Custom cake deposits are refundable up to 7 days before the date.

Do you deliver to venues?

Yes. For weddings and large events we deliver and set up the cake at the venue, within our delivery area.
