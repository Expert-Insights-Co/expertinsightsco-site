Questions

How far do you deliver?

Delivery is included within about 25 miles of central El Paso. Areas up to 40 miles away, including Las Cruces, have a delivery fee. The booking builder checks your ZIP automatically.

What if it rains or gets too windy?

Inflatables can't be used in high wind or rain. If we have to cancel for weather, your deposit is refunded or moved to a new date, whichever you prefer.

Do I need to be home for setup?

Someone 18 or older needs to be there at setup to show us where things go and sign the safety agreement. Pickup doesn't require anyone at home.

Can I change my order after booking?

Yes. Add items or change the date online until 6 days before the party. After that, call or text us and we'll do our best.
