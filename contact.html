How it works

  1. An initial conversation. We learn who will attend and what you want to change.
  2. A written proposal. You receive the agenda, format and fee for your review.
  3. The session. Delivered in person anywhere in the U.S., or virtually.
  4. Follow-up. We share the participant evaluation, a brief summary and recommended next steps.

Keynotes run 60 to 90 minutes, workshops 2 to 4 hours, and series meet over several weeks. Public-sector and education rates are available.
